HD downloader — quick answers

Why does one video show HD and another only SD?

HD only exists if the video was uploaded in HD. If the creator uploaded in SD, no downloader can recover quality that was never there.

Is 1080p always better than 720p?

On a big screen, yes. On a phone, 720p often looks just as sharp and downloads faster with a smaller file.

Does HD cost extra on VidFetch?

No. HD options are shown in the same free flow as SD and MP3 — the page is supported by its labelled ad slots, not by paywalling quality.
